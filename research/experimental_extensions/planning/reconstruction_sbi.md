**Scientific scope and primary evidence.**

[RECCE, Cao et al., CVPR 2022](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Cao_End-to-End_Reconstruction-Classification_Learning_for_Face_Forgery_Detection_CVPR_2022_paper.html) combines genuine-image reconstruction, supervised classification, multiscale encoder/decoder graph features and reconstruction guidance. A small CAE plus residual concatenation is RECCE-inspired, not a reproduction of that complete architecture. The [author implementation](https://github.com/VISION-SJTU/RECCE) confirms the genuine-reconstruction motivation.

[SBI, Shiohara and Yamasaki, CVPR 2022](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Shiohara_Detecting_Deepfakes_With_Self-Blended_Images_CVPR_2022_paper.html) constructs pseudo source and target images from one pristine face and blends them to induce inconsistencies. It is a justified priority for face manipulation generalization. It should not be described as the strongest universal generalization method: [LSDA, Yan et al., CVPR 2024](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Yan_Transcending_Forgery_Specificity_with_Latent_Space_Augmentation_for_Generalizable_Deepfake_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf) explicitly evaluates weaknesses of pixel blending under perturbations and whole-face synthesis. [Video blending and adapter tuning, Yan et al., CVPR 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Yan_Generalizing_Deepfake_Video_Detection_with_Plug-and-Play_Video-Level_Blending_and_Spatiotemporal_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf) finds spatial and temporal synthesis complementary. Those results motivate preserving generic representations and reporting per-technique results; temporal adaptation requires video structure absent locally and is deferred.

[UniversalFakeDetect, Ojha et al., CVPR 2023](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Ojha_Towards_Universal_Fake_Image_Detectors_That_Generalize_Across_Generative_Models_CVPR_2023_paper.html) motivates a cheap generic-pretrained feature/linear-probe control. Its task and data differ from this benchmark, so its reported improvements are not predictions for MFFI.

**One fit, resume and provenance interface.**

The lead should extend or extract the audited engine into one reusable `fit(config, task, train_loader, val_loader, resume=...)`. A task supplies `training_step(batch, state)` returning scalar total loss, named component losses and optional logits; parameter groups; validation metrics with a declared selection key/direction; and checkpoint reconstruction. Detectors expose `forward(raw_rgb) -> logits`; reconstruction models expose `reconstruct(raw_rgb) -> {reconstruction, mu?, logvar?, latent}`. The adapter owns genuine-only filtering and losses. The runner owns AMP, accumulation, optimizer/scheduler, clipping, resource reporting and atomic save/load.

Retain exact **completed-epoch** resume first. Save model, optimizer, scheduler, AMP scaler, completed epoch, successful optimizer-update count, beta schedule definition/state, best selection metric, history, Python/NumPy/Torch RNG and loader/sampling seed. Advance beta only on successful optimizer updates, not microbatches. Average each accumulation window by its actual sample count, including the final partial window. Epoch-keyed SBI randomness makes replay of an interrupted epoch deterministic. Explicitly document that interrupted partial epochs restart from the last completed epoch; do not claim arbitrary-step exact resume.

Run identity must include architecture/loss/schedule settings, manifest hashes, accepted landmark cohort, image/cache hashes, initialization checkpoint and generic-pretraining source, preprocessing, code revision, seed, selection policy and augmentation recipe. Record `initialization_exposure=generic_pretrained|mffi_supervised|scratch` and `training_fake_source=none|sbi|mffi|mixed`. An AE pretraining completion certificate needs reconstruction metrics, not classifier calibration; downstream detector certificates retain validation predictions and frozen calibration. New outputs use the existing audited metrics/artifact helpers.

**Minimal components for A-D.**

| Component | Initial design and scientific control |
|---|---|
| CAE | Four strided convolution stages, widths 32/64/128/256, GroupNorm and a compact bottleneck; bilinear upsampling plus convolutions; sigmoid RGB output. Local resolution 128, server resolution configurable including 224. No encoder-to-decoder skips. |
| V-CAE | Same encoder/decoder budget; latent dimension 128; `mu` and bounded `logvar`; reparameterize during training and use `z=mu` at evaluation. Log analytic KL separately from its weighted contribution. |
| Gated deep-skip CAE | Only an H/8 by W/8 skip by default, with a learned sigmoid gate and no H, H/2 or H/4 skip. Enforce a configurable maximum no shallower than H/4. Compare with the same decoder with gates disabled. |
| ResidualFusionBlock | Compute `[x, x_hat, abs(x-x_hat)]` in raw RGB for exactly nine channels; optional one-channel Sobel magnitude of grayscale absolute residual gives ten. Fix and record derivative scaling. Normalize each three-channel segment explicitly after fusion. |
| Spatial detector | Generic-pretrained ResNet-18, with a nine/ten-channel stem initialized to reproduce its RGB-only stem by copying RGB weights and zeroing added channels. Compare against the same backbone with RGB alone. Root `classifier` or explicit parameter groups. |
| Latent detector | MLP on `[mu, logvar, KL_per_sample]`, giving `2d+1` inputs. Fit feature normalization on train only. Optional per-dimension KL is diagnostic before adding redundant features. Compare frozen VAE latent-only, spatial-only and their simple mean. |
| Genuine-only anomaly baseline | Mean raw-RGB L1 reconstruction error, with declared larger-is-faker polarity. Select AE by held-out real reconstruction loss. Preserve raw scores; for audited probability-shaped outputs use a fixed monotone sigmoid standardized by training-real error location/scale and label it an anomaly score, not a calibrated probability. |

Restricted skips are a controlled architectural hypothesis, not proof that all manipulation information is removed. [Reverse distillation, Deng and Li, CVPR 2022](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Deng_Anomaly_Detection_via_Reverse_Distillation_From_One-Class_Embedding_CVPR_2022_paper.html) motivates compact normal-data embeddings, but does not establish an H/4 boundary for faces. [Yong and Brintrup, 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.12637) also shows that bottleneck restrictions are not universally beneficial. Test the specified shallow-path exclusion and gate-disabled equivalence; decide usefulness from validation and complementarity.

AE pretraining uses only the 202 train reals, never validation reals. Default detector training freezes the AE, including its training/evaluation mode. Optional fine-tuning updates the AE from reconstruction loss on real examples only while the classifier consumes detached reconstructions; use separate `lr_ae`, `lr_backbone`, `lr_head`. If a later ablation lets fake-example classification gradients update the AE, name it supervised joint adaptation and stop describing the final AE as trained only on genuine inputs.

`CompositeReconstructionLoss` returns total plus L1, SSIM, LPIPS, raw KL and beta. Use pixel-mean L1; batch-mean `1-SSIM`; and analytic `KL_i = 0.5 * sum(mu_i**2 + exp(logvar_i) - 1 - logvar_i)`. Document KL summed over latent dimensions before averaging the batch, since otherwise beta changes meaning with latent dimension. Compute KL and SSIM in float32 under AMP. Implement Gaussian-window SSIM in Torch with declared `[0,1]` data range, using the [authors' SSIM definition](https://ece.uwaterloo.ca/~z70wang/research/ssim/). Start with L1 plus 0.1 SSIM; VAE beta maximum 1e-4 is a proposed starting scale, not a validated setting.

Support linear warm-up and cyclic warm-up with explicit total updates, cycle count and ramp fraction. [Fu et al., NAACL 2019](https://aclanthology.org/N19-1021/) studies cyclical beta for NLP VAEs, so benefit for convolutional face VAEs remains an ablation. Default to one linear ramp over 30% of planned updates; smoke-test cyclic boundaries/resume and defer its expensive comparison. LPIPS is opt-in, versioned and frozen, with image gradients enabled and inputs mapped to `[-1,1]` as specified by the [author package](https://github.com/richzhang/PerceptualSimilarity). Both learned LPIPS weights and its feature backbone must be explicitly available offline. Missing dependencies/assets must fail before training; do not silently set the coefficient to zero or replace pretrained features with random weights.

**SBI data and offline landmark design.**

The train manifest was independently counted: 1,000 examples, including 202 reals. Visual inspection of the first three lexicographically sorted real filenames (`034d766...`, `03732e...`, `04b881...`) found centered face crops with varied background, glasses and partial hair occlusion. This is only a visual preflight, not a landmark success-rate measurement.

Use optional **MediaPipe Tasks FaceLandmarker**, explicit CPU delegate, local `.task` asset, IMAGE mode, detection/presence thresholds 0.5 and `num_faces=2` so ambiguity is detectable. The [official interface](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_landmarker/python) accepts local model assets and returns normalized mesh coordinates; [BaseOptions](https://ai.google.dev/edge/api/mediapipe/python/mp/tasks/BaseOptions) exposes CPU execution. Cache once in a separate low-thread process. Pin package/model hashes after installation feasibility is checked; no automatic download during training.

Use a named face-oval index set and convex hull for the blend mask. This differs from the [original SBI release's dlib 81-point preprocessing](https://github.com/mapooon/SelfBlendedImages), so name the recipe `sbi_mediapipe_v1`, not an exact SBI reproduction. Implement the paper's mechanism independently; do not vendor the release's research-restricted code. If MediaPipe fails the preflight, evaluate an explicitly selected `face_alignment` CPU backend with separately staged weights, rather than silently switching landmark topology.

Cache records contain sample/image hashes, original dimensions, landmark coordinates/topology, model/package hashes, options and one explicit status: `ok`, `no_face`, `multiple_faces`, `invalid_geometry`, or `unreadable`. Verify finite coordinates, plausible hull area and bounds. Missing/stale records fail training. Optional exclusion requires a separately materialized accepted manifest and failure report with counts; use the same accepted real cohort for paired comparators. Never invent a central ellipse or replace a failed image with another one.

For every accepted real, derive pristine and pseudo-fake siblings from that same image. Apply independently sampled color/contrast/sharpness/resampling and small affine/elastic mask perturbations, soften the boundary, and compute `fake = alpha * source + (1-alpha) * target`. Apply post-blend JPEG/resize identically in distribution to both classes. Derive randomness from `(seed, epoch, base_sample_id, view)` using the audited `sample_seed`; preserve `base_sample_id` as the grouping key. Keep siblings in the same split and use balanced paired batches without additional class weights. Predecoded images and cached masks avoid repeated detection/JPEG decoding; start at zero workers under CPU contention. Validation SBI views have a frozen seed/recipe. Evaluation of actual targets uses their unmodified images and needs no landmarks.

**Leakage-aware SBI protocol.**

| Initialization and training | Permitted interpretation |
|---|---|
| Generic ImageNet MobileNetV3 or ResNet-18, new head, train reals plus SBI only | No MFFI fake images used for fitting; useful local unseen-training-forgery proxy. Record generic weight identity and limits of unknown pretraining exposure. |
| Same generic initialization, matched optimizer/budget, reals plus MFFI fakes | Supervised control. Add a mixture arm only after the two main arms complete. |
| MFFI HF backbone, then reals plus SBI | SBI adaptation after MFFI fake exposure. Never call this unseen-forgery training, even if the old head is reset or backbone frozen. |

Selection on actual MFFI validation fakes is permitted by the project protocol but makes the clean arm **source-validation selected**, not a wholly MFFI-fake-unseen procedure. A strict supplementary arm selects/calibrates on frozen SBI from validation reals and evaluates MFFI fakes once only after freezing. State which selection protocol was used. Do not borrow validation reals for AE/SBI gradient training. Repeated synthetic views of 202 faces do not increase the number of independent source faces. Missing identity/video metadata prevents identity-level generalization claims.

Server priority is SBI adaptation of verified MobileNet-SRM first for cost, then CLIP-SRM/DINO-SRM, alongside the generic-pretrained clean comparator. For each frozen survivor the same audited evaluation runs Test, Test-D, DF-40 and Celeb-DF v2, with source-validation calibration, mean frame scores for videos and declared fake-is-1. No local DF-40 or Celeb-DF claim is possible.

SBI gets equal-step generic-pretrained SBI-only versus MFFI-supervised pilots, not an expensive backbone sweep. Use validation only until configurations are frozen.
4. Stop extra compute immediately for nonfinite loss/gradients, collapsed score variance, corrupted sample identity, landmark coverage failures, or memory pressure. Stop scaling reconstruction if it fails to reconstruct a tiny training batch, or if validation anomaly/spatial scores remain near chance and their simple fusion with existing experts adds no benefit. AUC gaps below about 0.03 on the 1,000-image validation set are inconclusive, not evidence of superiority. Report paired bootstrap intervals, score correlation, disagreement and fixed mean-fusion gain. Low correlation alone is not useful diversity. Do not reject the entire reconstruction concept merely because raw error fails; supervised residual and latent paths remain implemented.
